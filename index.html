Plug in the table mic before starting. On Android a USB mic usually becomes the default on its own. Mic names appear after the first Start.

Background noise filter

The tablet's own noise clean-up. Try both at your table; Off sometimes gives the caption service a truer signal. A saved recording keeps whichever was used.

Helps with names the service would otherwise mishear. Up to 50.

Reading

Words on screen

"As they're heard" is quickest but words can change as the service catches up. "Settled only" waits about a second and never rewrites.

Who's speaking

Telling voices apart from one mic is the least reliable part. If the labels are wrong more often than right, switch them off and keep the words.

Voices are numbered in the order they're heard. Numbering starts over after a reconnect or restart.

Save recording

Keeps the very same audio that's sent to the caption service, as a WAV file, written to this tablet every 20 seconds. It takes effect at the next Start and uses about 115 MB an hour. Let everyone at the table know they're being recorded.

Plays a saved recording through the live caption service as if it were the mic, so you can compare settings on the same audio. Uses Deepgram for now.
